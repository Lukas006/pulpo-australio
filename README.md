# pulpo-australio

Secret spots, off-grid adventure itineraries, and curated travel guides across Australia.

- **About Australia: The Land Down Under** (Country Spotlight) — 65,000+ years of history milestones, key country statistics, all proclaimed national and state/territory flags with SVG artwork, and celebrated Australian icons (from Steve Irwin to Cathy Freeman).
- **Melbourne: Culture, Coffee & Hidden Laneways** (Free Digital Guide) — Complete insider city itinerary featuring secret laneways, specialty coffee roasters, Royal Botanic Gardens, Queen Victoria Market, St Kilda fairy penguins, Southbank arts precinct, and Albert Park (Formula 1 Grand Prix circuit).
- **Lord Howe Island** (NSW Secret Spot)
- **Karijini National Park Gorges** (WA Secret Spot)
- **Hinchinbrook Island** (QLD Secret Spot)
- **The Kimberley Unlocked** (Digital Guide & GPX)
- **Tasmania Wild & Unfiltered** (Digital Guide & GPX)
