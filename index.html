Hidden Gems & Secret Spots

Uncrowded wonderlands known mainly to locals and expert explorers.

WA Secret

Horizontal Falls (Kimberley)

A natural phenomenon where massive 12-meter tides force seawater rapidly through narrow coastal gorges.

UNESCO Secret

Lord Howe Island

A secluded paradise limiting visitors to just 400 at a time to preserve its volcanic peaks and coral lagoon.

SA Secret

Lake MacDonnell (Pink Lake)

A striking natural pink lake separated from a turquoise ocean bay by a single narrow dirt road.
