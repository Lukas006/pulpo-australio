NSW Secret Spot

Lord Howe Island Secrets

About This Spot

Situated in the Tasman Sea, Lord Howe limits tourist numbers to just 400 at any time. It boasts world-class snorkeling around the southernmost coral reef on Earth.

Pulpo Insider Tips

  • Hike Mt. Gower with a mandatory local guide to see ancient cloud forests.
  • Hand-feed wild kingfish right at Ned's Beach at low tide.

Location Specs

Best Season: Sep – May

Permit Required: Yes (Flight capped)

Cell Service: Minimal / No 5G

WA Secret Spot

Karijini National Park Gorges

About This Spot

2.5 billion years in the making, Karijini offers spider-walks through layered red gorges leading to crystal-clear natural swimming pools.

Pulpo Insider Tips

  • Visit Hancock Gorge early in the morning to beat tour groups.
  • Bring sturdy aquatic shoes for slippery canyon walks.

Location Specs

Best Season: May – Sep

Vehicle Access: 4WD Recommended
